A LAND OF QUIET WONDERS

Meet Wayanad.
Stay for the feeling.

Set high in the Western Ghats of northern Kerala, Wayanad is a landscape of mist-covered mountains, coffee and spice plantations, ancient rock carvings, forest trails and cascading waterfalls. It is a place to slow down, breathe deeply and experience nature at its own pace.

From the prehistoric stories of Edakkal Caves to the calm waters of Pookode Lake and the sweeping views at Banasura Sagar, every corner has something worth discovering.

BEYOND THE POSTCARD

Let the forest set the pace.

Wake up to birdsong, follow a trail through rain-fed greenery, pause beside a waterfall and end the day with the last light spilling over the hills. Wayanad is best explored slowly, with care for the land and the communities who call it home.
